================================================================================
7. LESSONS LEARNED
================================================================================

- Encoding schemes such as Hex and Base64 do not provide cryptographic
  security and can be reversed directly.
- Multiple encoding layers should be identified and removed sequentially.
- A single-byte XOR key provides very weak confidentiality and can be
  recovered through known plaintext or brute-force analysis.
- The presence of readable Base64 characters after Hex decoding is a strong
  indication that another encoding layer is present.
- The final output should always be validated against the expected flag format.
